Hold R when the purple arc beside the crosshair is full to ReWind — up to 15 seconds of the run play backwards, XP and powerups included. Let go anywhere to resume from there after a 3-2-1 countdown.

SHIFT to dash — a shove in the direction you are facing, spending one of your dash charges. Charges refill over time.
